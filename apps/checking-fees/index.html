These are three large banks with branches in New Jersey. The table shows the standard checking account at each one: Chase Total Checking, Bank of America Advantage Plus Banking, and TD Bank Convenience Checking. Fees were checked in November 2026. Use this table for the whole assignment.

Reminder: Use only the fees in this table. Assume every overdraft is big enough to be charged, and ignore overdraft protection plans. Ignore the owner's surcharge that some ATMs add.

Worked Example (Guided Practice)

How to show your math: List each fee, explain whether it applies, then add the amounts.

Dani banks at Chase. This month she has $380 in direct deposits, her balance never reaches $1,500, she uses an out-of-network ATM 1 time, and she has no overdrafts.

FeeHow we figure it outAmount
Monthly maintenance fee$380 is less than $500 and her balance stays under $1,500, so the fee is NOT waived.$15
ATM fees1 withdrawal x $3$3
Overdraft fees0 overdrafts$0
Total for the month$15 + $3 + $0$18

Over 12 months: $18 x 12 = $216 in fees.
